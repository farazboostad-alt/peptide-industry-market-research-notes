
# Peptide Industry Market Research Notes

This repository contains general research resources for understanding the peptide industry and its business environment.

It focuses on market research, customer needs, brand positioning, and business planning.

## Research Areas

- Industry overview
- Customer research
- Competitor positioning
- Market trends
- Business opportunities
- Brand development

## Market Research Framework

### 1. Understand the Industry
Review the market structure and identify the main business categories.

### 2. Study Customer Needs
Understand common questions, expectations, and purchasing considerations.

### 3. Review Competitors
Compare business positioning, service offerings, and communication approaches.

### 4. Identify Opportunities
Find areas where businesses can improve their products, services, and customer experience.

### 5. Organize Findings
Document useful research and update it as the market changes.
